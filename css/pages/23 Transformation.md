# Transformation

## Translate
This can translate/slide on the x, y, or both axes
* `translateX(x)` translates on x axis
* `translateY(y)` translates on y axis
* `translate(x, y)` translates on both axes

## Rotate
This can rotate/flip an item while it never moves
* `rotatex(deg)` rotates on x axis
* `rotatey(deg)` rotates on y axis
* `rotatez(deg)` rotates on z and doesnt flip but rotates in the expected sense

## Scale
This makes something larger or smaller on a given axis by 100% (example scalex(1.5) scales it 150% biger on the x axis)

* `scalex(%)` scales on the x axis
* `scaley(%)` scales on the y axis
* `scaley(%, %)` scales on both axes

## Skew
Skew slants something on either the x or y axis

* `skewx(deg)` skews on x axis
* `skewy(deg)` skews on y axis
* `skewx(deg, deg)` skews on both axes
