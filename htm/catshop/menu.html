Chapter 1:

Assigning Monetary Value to Your Cat's Apperance

Depending on your kingdom these laws will Differ


Dull Gwynedd

To have value, a cat must be able to hear and see, and it must have claws as well as catch mice, give birth, and not eat their offspring.


Dull Dyfed

Includes all of Dull Gwynedds rules, as well as having good teeth and not wail at the moon.


Dull Gwent

All rules of Dull Gwynedd and Dull Dylfed, as well as having no burn marks and having a good tail.


Monetary value

One Adult cat (assuming it follows all the rules of your kingdom) costs 4 Ceiniog. However, in Gwyneed, you can purchase a kitten that has not opened its eyes for one Ceiniog, and you can purchase a cat that has opened its eyes at 2 Ceiniog, and once that cat starts catching mice, it is then valued at 4 Ceiniog.
However, if you attempt to scam your coustomer then one-third of The Cat's value is to be returned
